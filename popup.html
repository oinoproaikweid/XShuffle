X returns about 15 posts per search, so a wider window finds older posts but searches more. Auto sizes the window from how often the account posts.
Rate limits
1s
Milliseconds to wait before each retry search. Spacing searches out makes X rate-limiting you less likely; 0 searches as fast as possible.
When X rate-limits you, Xshuffle pauses itself rather than searching again and making it worse. Turn this on to search through that pause. Doing so can deepen the limit or extend how long it lasts.
